AFIT — Privacy Policy

AI-Powered Fitness Coaching · Android and iPhone

Effective date: June 22, 2026  |  Last updated: October 21, 2026

AFIT ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered fitness and nutrition coaching app for Android and iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using AFIT you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase), or, if you choose Google sign-in or Sign in with Apple, the name and email address that provider shares with us. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; we then only receive Apple's private relay address.
  • Fitness profile: age, sex, weight, height, training goals, experience level, equipment, injuries, and dietary preferences — entered during onboarding or in settings.
  • Workout logs and nutrition entries you record in the app.
  • Messages you send to the AI coach.
  • Photos you choose to analyse: when you use AI food logging or form feedback, the photo you take or pick is sent for analysis. AFIT only opens the camera or photo library when you tap to do so.

Collected automatically:

  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, your device's push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging, delivered through Apple Push Notification service on iPhone) so we can send training reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Health & activity data (Android only, optional, on-device only):

  • If you grant permission, AFIT reads step count, heart rate, sleep duration, and calorie data from Android Health Connect.
  • This data is read directly from Health Connect on your device. It is used only to personalise your dashboard and AI coaching insights. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
  • You can revoke this permission at any time via Android Settings > Apps > AFIT > Permissions, or via the Health Connect app.
  • The iPhone version of AFIT does not access Apple Health data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service: generate personalised training programmes, track workouts, log meals, and deliver AI coaching responses.
  • To power AI features: AFIT asks for your permission before its first AI request. With your permission, the information a feature needs — your fitness profile, your questions, and any photo you choose — is sent through our secure server-side proxy to our AI providers, OpenAI (via OpenRouter) and Groq, to generate your response. Your name and email address are not sent. The providers process it only to produce your response, and it is not used for advertising.
  • To send reminders: training and inactivity reminders, if you allow notifications.
  • To improve the app: we may review aggregated, anonymised usage patterns to fix bugs and improve features. We do not sell or share individual user data.
  • To send transactional emails: account confirmation and password reset (via Supabase Auth).

AFIT does not show ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. AI coaching is general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Your account and fitness data are stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database with row-level security. Only you can access your own data.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — Supabase uses bcrypt hashing.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.
  • AI requests are routed through a server-side proxy, so AI provider keys are never exposed to your device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — authentication and database hosting.
  • OpenRouter / OpenAI (openrouter.ai, openai.com) and Groq (groq.com) — AI processing of coaching requests and photos you choose to analyse, as described in section 2.
  • Google Firebase (firebase.google.com) — delivery of push notifications.
  • Google and Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Health Data (Android Health Connect)

AFIT complies with the Android Health Connect Permissions Policy. Specifically:

  • Health Connect data is accessed only with your explicit permission.
  • We read steps, heart rate, sleep, and active calories solely to display them on your dashboard and to inform AI coaching.
  • We do not upload Health Connect data to any server.
  • We do not share Health Connect data with any third party, including advertisers.
  • We do not use Health Connect data for advertising purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

AFIT is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & export: you can view all data you have entered in the app at any time.
  • Delete account: in the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and permanently. You can also email u55d55@gmail.com and we will action it within 30 days.
  • AI features: you choose whether to allow AI data sharing when you first use an AI feature.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Health permissions (Android): revoke at any time via Android Settings without affecting other app functionality.
  • Marketing emails: we do not send marketing emails. All emails are transactional (account-related only).

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, your account and all associated personal data are deleted immediately; deletion requests by email are completed within 30 days. Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify you via the app or email before the changes take effect.
